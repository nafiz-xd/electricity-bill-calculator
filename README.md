# Electricity Bill Calculator ⚡

Calculate Bangladesh residential (LT-A) electricity bills the smart way — for **DESCO, DPDC, NESCO, BPDB & BREB** connections, using the BERC tariff slabs.

## 🔗 Live Demo
👉 https://nafiz-xd.github.io/electricity-bill-calculator/

## ✨ Smart Features
- **Slab-wise bill calculator** — enter monthly units and see exactly which slab each unit falls into, plus demand charge (৳42/kW), meter rent and 5% VAT, all itemised
- **Lifeline rule built in** — months of 50 units or less are billed at the ৳4.63 lifeline rate
- **Prepaid recharge planner** — enter a recharge amount and your daily average use; get estimated units and *how many days the recharge will last*
- **Appliance cost estimator** — fans, lights, TV, fridge, AC… set watt, quantity and hours/day to estimate monthly units, the bill, and your biggest consumer
- **Monthly history & chart** — save each month's bill and compare them in a bar chart with average/highest stats (saved in your browser)
- **Editable charges** — demand charge, meter rent and load are adjustable, so the tool stays useful when tariffs change

## 💡 Tariff used
BERC residential tariff (June 2026 revision): lifeline ৳4.63 · 1–75 ৳5.26 · 76–200 ৳8.50 · 201–300 ৳9.10 · 301–400 ৳9.62 · 401–600 ৳15.01 · 600+ ৳17.35, demand charge ৳42/kW, 5% VAT. Estimates only — always check your own bill for the latest rates.
